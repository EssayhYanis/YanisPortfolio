Unreal Engine Blueprints Niagara

My role / Gameplay Programmer & VFX

Developed by Almost a Dozen, an 11-person team at Algonquin College. Players choose a class and fight from Olympus to the Underworld in a world inspired by Greek mythology.

My contributions / Capstone II

Maps, abilities & visual effects.

My work covered map interfaces, specific ability implementations, Niagara effects, materials, and documentation, with iteration and rework support across multiple abilities.

01
Minimap, full map & pings

Created the minimap with SceneCapture2D, a render target and UI; the full map toggled with M; and middle-click pings. Built player and enemy map UI.

02
Ability implementations

Implemented Kiss of Life, Impunity, and Nature’s Blessing, plus the initial version of Piercing Arrow.

03
Niagara effects & materials

Created effects for several abilities, including War Banner, the Rage of the Gods aura, and Arrow VFX reused in Multi-Shot.

04
Project documentation

Worked on the MDD for the previous semester’s release and contributed to the Release 4 GDD.

Collaboration & implementation changes +

I created the initial networking for the minimap and pings; another programmer later refined it in C++. My credit is for the initial implementation.

Another programmer later reworked the player map UI, and a teammate reworked Piercing Arrow after my initial implementation. My Tank Charge VFX was later replaced by another team member’s effect. Colored pings and pinging directly on the map were attempted, then scrapped due to time constraints.

VFX contribution details +

My VFX work included War Banner, the Rage of the Gods aura, Arrow, Impunity, Moonbeam, Nature’s Blessing, Mirror of Desire, Kiss of Life, and Tank Charge, using Niagara systems and materials.

Tank Charge’s effect was later replaced. My separate Multi-Shot effect was not used; my Arrow VFX was reused instead. My ability programming contributions are listed above.
