About me

Hi, I’m Yanis.

Game developer based in Ottawa.

I was born and raised in Morocco, where my love for video games started as a kid. Whether it was on a phone, a friend’s PC, or a console, I took every chance I could to play.

Gaming and anime have stayed a big part of my life. My friends and I still share that passion: playing together, talking about our favourite worlds, and imagining the games we’d like to make ourselves.

In 2023, I moved to Canada to study Game Development at Algonquin College. I now live in Ottawa, Ontario. Studying programming gave me a way to start turning that childhood interest into something I could build.

I want to create games that stay with people—the kind they remember years later and share with their friends. My hope is that something I make can inspire another player, young or old, the way games inspired me.
